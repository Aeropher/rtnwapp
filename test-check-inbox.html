Now check your inbox

I've sent you an email with a confirmation link in it. Click that link and you're done — until you do, your address doesn't reach me and I can't add you to the test.

It should arrive within a minute or two, from aeropherhq@gmail.com. If it isn't there, check your spam folder — a solo developer's first email to you is exactly the sort of thing filters are suspicious of.

Then what?

  1. I add you to the tester list in the Play Console. That's me, at a keyboard, so give it a day or two.
  2. Google emails you an opt-in link of its own. Accept it, then install RightNow from Play like any other app.

Nothing arrived at all, or you've realised you typed the wrong address? Sign up again with the right one, or just email me and I'll sort it out.
