Put me on the Android test

I need the email address your phone uses for the Google Play Store — not whichever address you use most.

Not sure which it is? On your phone, open the Play Store and tap your profile picture in the top right. The address shown there is the one I need.

Play matches its tester list against that exact account. Give me a different address and the invitation quietly goes nowhere — you never get in, and neither of us finds out why.

You'll get a confirmation email first — click the link in it or your address never reaches me. It goes to Buttondown, which runs my mailing list, and then to the Play Console so I can add you to the test. You'll also get the occasional post about building RightNow, with an unsubscribe link in every one.
