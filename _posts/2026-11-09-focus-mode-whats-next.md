Focus mode is the full-screen view you get when you tap **Focus** on a task on Today. It's just that task, a timer and its subtasks, with nothing else on screen.

Until now, finishing the task sent you straight back to Today, where you'd have to look at the list, pick the next thing and tap **Focus** again. If you're in the middle of getting stuff done, that's the point where you're most likely to wander off.

## What happens now

When you tap the tick to finish a task, you get a screen that says what you've just done, then shows the next task on Today in a big card underneath. Tap it, or **Start**, and you're in focus mode on that task with the timer back at zero.

Below the card is a smaller **Take a break**, which takes you back to Today. I made the next task bigger on purpose, because carrying on should be the easy option. Stopping is still only one tap away.

The next task is whatever's top of your Today list once you take out what's done, so it follows your order, whether you've dragged tasks into place or let priority sort them. If there's nothing left, it says so and gives you **Back to Today** instead.

## Two fixes while I was in there

Finishing a task in focus mode was a slightly lesser version of ticking it off on Today. Two things were wrong with it:

- The task stayed marked as the one you were working on, even after it was done.
- Any subtasks you added while in focus mode were lost when you finished, because RightNow saved the task as it was when you went in.

Both are fixed.
