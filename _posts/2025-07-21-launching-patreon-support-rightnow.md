After 6 months of building RightNow in public and countless hours of "vibe coding" with AI assistants, I'm excited to announce that I'm launching a Patreon to help sustain and accelerate development of the capacity-first productivity app that's changing how we think about task management.

## Why Patreon?

Building an indie app while working full-time is a labor of love, but it also comes with real costs - server hosting, app store fees, testing devices, and most importantly, time. Your support will help me:

- Keep RightNow free for core features
- Accelerate development of iOS and desktop versions
- Add premium features like cloud sync and team collaboration
- Dedicate more focused time to the project

## What You'll Get

Sometimes the best support is knowing someone believes in what you're building. For the price of a coffee (or half a coffee in London), you'll get:

- **My eternal gratitude** and a virtual high five 🙌

- **Monthly behind-the-scenes updates** on development progress, challenges, and wins
- **Early access to blog posts** before they go public
- **Access to the Patron-only Discord channel** where we discuss features, productivity philosophy, and share task management tips

For those who want to shape RightNow's future:

- Everything from Tier 1, plus...

- **Bug bounty rewards** - find bugs, earn app credits
- **1-on-1 monthly office hours** (first 20 patrons)
- **Name in the app credits** as a founding supporter

## Why Support RightNow?

RightNow isn't just another todo app. It's built on a simple philosophy: **respect your human limits**. 

With 44,724 lines of carefully crafted Dart code, 258 passing tests, and a growing community of users who've discovered that doing less (but finishing it) beats an endless list of guilt-inducing tasks, RightNow is solving a real problem.

Your support helps an indie developer (that's me!) continue building something that matters. No VC funding, no growth-at-all-costs mentality - just sustainable development of a tool that helps people work better.

## What's Next?

- **More gamification** - because grooming your backlog should be fun
