It's slowed development a bit, but the code quality is so much better.</p><p>If you're building your own projects, I'd recommend trying this prompt-driven approach. It's like having a conversation with your future self - "Hey Alex, here's all the stuff you thought of but didn't have time to implement yet." Plus, when you sit down to code, you don't waste time wondering what to work on!</p><p>Keep an eye on this space - I'll probably share more prompt collections as development continues. It's quite cathartic seeing all those [DONE] markers!</p>
